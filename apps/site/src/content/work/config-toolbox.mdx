Zuora customers wanted pages the company couldn't hand-build for everyone — from
adding a section to an existing page, to standing up whole applications for their
own subscriber management. Config Toolbox was the homegrown no-code answer: let
users shape the Zuora UI themselves.

## The problem

Demand was wide-ranging and bespoke, and the team couldn't manually service it
customer by customer. People needed to add, modify, and remove information across
the product on their own terms.

> **The challenge:** build a no-code solution that lets users edit existing Zuora
> UI — adding, changing, or removing what they need.

## Approach

We anchored the tool to **Custom Objects**, an existing Zuora feature for
user-defined data objects, so configuration mapped to real data. Research
synthesis pushed the design from a complex inline page editor toward a far
simpler left-panel, toggle-based model: field toggling and modification, content
addition, access management, linking, and visibility controls — for both list and
detail pages.

## Outcome

The MVP earned strong user validation and became a lever for bigger change: it
advocated for a company-wide product redesign, opened design-system
implementation discussions, prompted a move away from server-side rendering, and
helped spark cross-functional redesign initiatives.

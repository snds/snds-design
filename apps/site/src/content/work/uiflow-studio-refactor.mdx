Uiflow Studio is a no-code editor for building applications. But its most
powerful capabilities — logic and live data — were hard to find and cramped once
you got there. With a roadmap that would relocate several features anyway, a
comprehensive refactor was the logical move: make logic and data first-class,
and let the UI scale with what was coming.

## The problem

Users struggled to even locate the logic and data tools, and the workspace was
unnecessarily restrictive once they did. Moving between data connections and
logic created constant friction — and upcoming features needed room the current
layout didn't have.

> **The challenge:** improve the accessibility and usability of key features, and
> adjust the UI to scale with the roadmap.

## Approach

Weekly hour-long sessions with beta customers and product partners made the
direction clear: users wanted a **dedicated workspace for logic workflows** with
a simple UI preview, rather than fighting the visual editor for space — and they
wanted to stop bouncing between the data-connection and logic areas.

## Outcome

The refactor gave logic workflows and data connections their own dedicated
spaces, cutting context-switching and lifting efficiency. Project completion time
dropped 32%, the work generated five new leads, re-engaged three lapsed users,
and brought in three new customers — with a warm reception at SaaStr.
